REMOREMO Privacy Policy

1. SCOPE AND CONTACT

This policy explains information handling for REMOREMO Client, REMOREMO Host, the Signaling Service used to establish connections, and this website. The developer responsible for the processing described here is Motoki Furusawa, an individual developer in Japan. Privacy questions and requests may be sent to motoki.furusawa@gmail.com. Do not send private keys, pairing secrets or computer passwords.

2. INFORMATION ON YOUR DEVICES AND IN REMOTE SESSIONS

The apps store device identity keys, saved connection settings, pairing and consent information, and local diagnostic records on your devices. The Host also stores trusted-device and revocation records. Keys and saved passwords use operating-system security facilities such as Keychain. Private identity signing keys and saved computer passwords are not sent to the Signaling Service.

Native REMOREMO sessions are designed to use encrypted connections between your devices. The Signaling Service does not relay or store their screen pixels, keyboard input or mouse input. Websites, VPN services and RDP/VNC servers you choose have their own data paths and policies.

Local diagnostic files are not automatically uploaded to the developer. REMOREMO has no advertising, third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. If you contact support, we receive your email address, message and any information or diagnostic files you choose to send. Email providers also process that correspondence.

3. CONNECTION-SETUP INFORMATION

The Signaling Service, hosted on Cloudflare, processes Host public-key fingerprints, device public keys and key identifiers, invitation and request identifiers, signatures, nonces, timestamps, network candidates and related connection-setup messages. Network candidates may contain local or public addresses and ports. Cloudflare receives connecting IP addresses; the service also uses IP-derived hashes for request and connection limits. These hashes are not treated as anonymous data.

Ordinary connection rooms persist Host and device-key identifiers and related presence, replay-protection, request-limit and pending-connection state in hosted storage. Invitation rooms handle information associated with new-device connection requests. REMOREMO does not require a developer-operated account or a name or email address to pair devices.

4. PURPOSES AND LEGAL BASES

We use information to provide connections, recognize and authenticate devices, manage service resources, protect security, prevent replay and misuse, respond to requests, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not use connection identifiers for advertising or user profiling.

Where EU or UK data-protection law applies, the relevant bases are performance of the requested service where processing is necessary for that contract, legitimate interests in operating and protecting the service and addressing misuse where those interests are not overridden by your rights, and compliance with applicable legal obligations. Where processing requires consent, that consent may be withdrawn without affecting earlier lawful processing. These bases apply only where their legal requirements are met.

5. RETENTION AND DELETION

Local records remain under the app's and operating system's storage controls until removed or replaced. Removing an app may leave Keychain items or Host support files. Revoking a device removes its authorization under the Host's trust rules; it does not by itself erase all historical service metadata.

Hosted device identifiers and associated security state can remain after a session ends. They are retained as service state for recognizing Hosts and paired devices and protecting connection setup until replaced or removed by subsequent service activity or cleanup. Ending a connection or uninstalling an app does not automatically remove those hosted records. Connection validity and request-limit windows are not deletion schedules.

Invitation cleanup clears its stored peer, nonce and IP-derived request records when the invitation is retired; a closed record remains to reject reuse. Provider operational records and backups follow the provider's own retention practices.

Retention decisions take account of the information's operational purpose, continuing device relationships, security and misuse risks, requests, legal requirements and the service's actual storage and cleanup behavior. Support correspondence may be retained to handle the request and related legal or security matters. This policy does not promise deletion of every record when a session ends or a request is made.

6. PROVIDERS AND INTERNATIONAL PROCESSING

Cloudflare provides signaling hosting and connection-reachability services. GitHub hosts this website and distributes the Host installer. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security. This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Apple provides the App Store and operating-system services. Email providers handle support correspondence. Websites, VPN providers and remote servers you use receive the requests you choose to send to them under their own policies.

Information may be processed in Japan and in other countries where the providers operate. Provider privacy policies explain their processing. Where applicable law requires international-transfer safeguards, those requirements apply. No particular storage country is promised.

7. SHARING, LEGAL REQUESTS AND SECURITY

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use remote-screen contents to train models. The service-provider processing described above is distinct from advertising-related sharing.

We may preserve or disclose information actually available to us where required by law or where lawful, necessary and proportionate to address unauthorized access, fraud or other misuse, protect rights or safety, or establish or defend legal claims. Recipients may include relevant service providers, professional advisers or competent authorities. This does not mean the Software identifies every user, conducts continuous investigations, or has access to remote-session contents through signaling.

The Software uses safeguards intended to reduce unauthorized access and disclosure, but no system provides absolute security. Protect your devices, credentials and pairing information.

8. YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

You can stop connections, disable the Host and revoke devices. For privacy questions or requests concerning information held by the developer, use the contact above. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent. You may complain to the relevant data-protection or supervisory authority.

Requests are handled in accordance with applicable law, including any required verification, deadlines, exceptions and rights of appeal. Verification should not require you to send private keys, pairing tokens or passwords. Deletion rights may be subject to security, legal and other lawful retention grounds. We do not waive your rights by describing storage or technical limits here.

9. CHILDREN AND POLICY CHANGES

REMOREMO is not directed at children. Contact us if you believe a child's information has been provided to us.

The official website is the current public source of this policy. We may update the policy to reflect actual processing and applicable law, with any notice or consent required by law. A policy update does not itself authorize a new incompatible use of personal information or replace consent where consent is required.
