REMOREMO Privacy Policy

Version 6.0 · Effective and last updated 8 November 2026.

1. SCOPE AND CONTACT

This policy describes REMOREMO for iPhone and iPad (Client), REMOREMO Host for macOS (Host), our connection-setup service (Signaling Service), and this REMOREMO legal website. It updates the information-handling description in the earlier REMOREMO policy. Installed applications may still display an earlier bundled copy. For privacy questions or requests concerning signaling metadata, contact motoki.furusawa@gmail.com. Do not send private keys, pairing QR codes, pairing tokens or computer passwords.

2. YOUR REMOTE SESSION

The Signaling Service helps devices authenticate and establish connections. It does not relay or store the remote screen pixels, keyboard input or mouse input of native REMOREMO sessions. Native sessions use authenticated, encrypted device-to-device connections. Supported internet connections use Direct P2P; there is no general media relay or TURN service. Direct reachability is not guaranteed on every network. RDP and VNC servers, VPN services and websites you choose have their own network and security settings.

3. INFORMATION ON YOUR DEVICES

The Host keeps its identity key in macOS Keychain, trusted-device and revocation state in local application storage, and pairing and consent information locally. Host identity keys may be Secure Enclave-backed or software keys protected by Keychain, depending on the identity originally created. The Client keeps device keys using the iOS security facilities, including Secure Enclave-backed authentication keys, and stores connection settings locally. Passwords you choose to save use the device Keychain. Private identity signing keys and saved computer passwords are not sent to the Signaling Service.

The apps keep local diagnostic records and consent information. REMOREMO does not automatically upload those local diagnostic files to the developer. If you voluntarily send diagnostic information for support, it is used to handle your request; remove secrets and unnecessary personal information before sending it. There is no advertising, third-party crash-reporting or analytics SDK in REMOREMO.

4. CONNECTION-SETUP INFORMATION

The Signaling Service runs on Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects. It processes Host public-key fingerprints, device public keys and key identifiers, signatures, request identifiers, nonces, timestamps, connection candidates and related setup messages to authenticate requests, prevent replay and abuse, and establish supported connections. Network candidates can contain local or public addresses and ports. Cloudflare receives connecting IP addresses. REMOREMO derives hashes from IP addresses for request and connection limits; those hashes are not anonymous information.

Ordinary Direct rooms persist Host fingerprints, trusted device-key identifiers, presence information, replay state, rate-limit state and pending connection metadata in Durable Object storage. A trusted-device identifier is an authentication identifier, rather than a claim that the developer knows your name. REMOREMO does not require a developer-operated user account or your name or email address to pair devices.

New-device pairing uses a separate invitation-scoped signaling room. The QR invitation includes information needed to reach and authenticate the Host and to prove possession of the invitation. The service processes invitation identifiers, expiry information, request proofs and connection-setup metadata. A valid QR alone does not register a device: device-key proof and the Mac owner's explicit approval are required. Your private device identity keys are not uploaded.

5. STORAGE AND RETENTION

Local records remain under the operating system's application storage and Keychain rules until removed or replaced through the relevant controls. Removing an app does not necessarily delete Keychain items or all Host support files. A software update does not require resetting keys or trust.

Ordinary signaling pending setup and replay records have bounded validity; the setup-message limit is at most 180 seconds. Request-rate decisions generally use a 60-second window. A validity window is not a promise of physical deletion at that time: some rate records are pruned when subsequent activity or cleanup runs. Host fingerprints and device-key identifiers can remain in ordinary Durable Object state after a session ends. There is no fixed automatic deletion deadline for every ordinary-room identifier in the current implementation; records may be replaced by later authenticated Host presence or other service cleanup.

Pairing invitations are valid for at most five minutes. When the invitation is closed or its expiry cleanup runs, the service clears stored peer identifiers, request nonces and IP-derived rate records from the invitation state and retires connection attachments. A closed marker remains to reject reuse. Cleanup is performed by service events and scheduled alarms, not a guarantee that a hosting provider erases every infrastructure copy at precisely 300 seconds.

Cloudflare and GitHub have their own operational and security logging and retention practices. We do not specify an independently verified fixed retention period or storage country for those provider records. Service-state cleanup described above does not promise immediate erasure of provider backups or logs.

6. SERVICE PROVIDERS AND THIS WEBSITE

Cloudflare hosts signaling and provides STUN reachability checks. Its infrastructure processes network requests under its own privacy terms. GitHub hosts the legal pages and distributes the Host installer. GitHub documents that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes even without GitHub sign-in. This website adds no analytics, advertising tags, tracking cookies, external fonts or JavaScript libraries. It uses local CSS and standard links.

Apple provides the App Store, operating systems, permissions and device authentication. Websites opened in the Client's browser receive your requests directly; its default starting page is Google. VPN services and RDP/VNC servers receive the connections you choose to make. Their own privacy terms apply. We do not operate those websites or servers.

7. PURPOSE, SHARING AND SECURITY

The information described here is used to operate connections, authenticate devices, prevent abuse and replay, and respond to support requests. We do not sell your information, use remote screen contents to train models, or use signaling identifiers for advertising or cross-app tracking. Processing by Cloudflare and GitHub is necessary for the hosted functions described above. Applicable law may require disclosure; this policy does not promise confidentiality against lawful process.

REMOREMO uses encrypted and authenticated native connections, operating-system key protection, limited-lifetime invitations and owner-approved trust registration. These controls do not eliminate every risk. Only approve pairing requests you initiated and protect your devices and pairing QR codes.

8. YOUR CHOICES, DELETION AND REQUESTS

You can stop connections, disable the Host, and revoke a device on the Mac. Revocation removes authorization under the Host's trust rules; it does not mean every historical server metadata record is immediately deleted. Host and Client local storage and Keychain are distinct from hosted signaling state. Reinstallation alone is not a reliable deletion method. Do not reset the Host identity or delete Keychain merely to upgrade.

To ask about access, correction or deletion of information, contact the support email above. The response and available rights depend on applicable law, the information actually held and a proportionate way to verify the request. There is no automated server-side account deletion control, and no blanket promise of immediate deletion of all provider logs. Do not send secrets as proof of ownership.

9. CHILDREN

REMOREMO is not directed at children. The existing Terms address permission for users who are not old enough to enter into an agreement under their local law.

10. CHANGES

We will publish policy updates here with an effective date. The copy bundled in an installed Host or Client may remain the version shipped with that application until it is updated. Publishing this policy does not update App Store privacy labels or the application binaries.
