REMOREMO CONNECTION AND DATA INFORMATION

This document describes the current implementation. It is technical information rather than a commitment to keep particular features, limits or timings unchanged. The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy address legal conditions and data handling.

CONNECTION ROUTES

Native REMOREMO connections can use a local TLS route or supported Direct P2P connections. A device being on Wi-Fi does not by itself mean signaling is unused: fallback may use Direct. Internet pairing needs compatible Client, Host and signaling versions and a network on which Direct P2P can be established. The current service exchanges connection-setup messages rather than relaying screen or input traffic. RDP and VNC connections use their selected servers and settings.

PAIRING AND SECURITY

The current QR invitation lasts up to 300 seconds. Its authentication, device-key proof, Host approval and trust-persistence steps precede a normal new-device session. Native transport protection depends on the route; operating-system key facilities also vary by device and the identity originally created. These details do not guarantee that a device, network or future version is free of vulnerabilities.

HOSTED STATE

Ordinary signaling persists Host fingerprints, trusted device-key identifiers, presence, replay, request-limit and pending state in Cloudflare Durable Objects. Some message-validity windows are bounded by 180 seconds and ordinary request-rate calculations generally use a 60-second window. These are not uniform storage-erasure deadlines. Expired rate records can remain until pruning runs, and ordinary device identifiers remain until replaced or removed as service state changes.

Invitation-room cleanup retires socket attachments and removes peer identifiers, request nonces and IP-derived records from its stored state while keeping a closed marker. Provider infrastructure records and backups are separate from application-state cleanup.

LOCAL STORAGE AND UPDATES

App support files, device keys and trust information have separate storage lifecycles. Reinstalling an app does not necessarily create a new identity or erase Keychain items. Use the product's device-management controls where appropriate. Updating is designed to preserve existing identity and trust information; do not delete those records merely to install an update.

SERVICE LIMITS

Connection and traffic limits may change for capacity, cost, technical or security reasons. No particular quota is specified by this document. Service-wide limits do not imply individual tracking or a finding that a particular user has misused the service.
